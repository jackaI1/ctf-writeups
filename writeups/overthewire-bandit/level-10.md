# Level 10: base64 text

**Goal:** `data.txt` is base64 encoded text.

## Steps

1. Decode with `base64 -d data.txt`. The password is the last word of the decoded sentence.

## What I learned

Base64 turns binary into letters and digits so it can travel in text. It is an encoding, not encryption, so anyone can decode it.
